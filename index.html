Key Technical Skills

01 · Research

AI Security & System-Level Reliability

I tackle AI security as a system problem. For 3D Gaussian Splatting, I redesigned the security analysis around the full pipeline, decomposed it stage by stage, and characterized how attacks surface across data, geometry, training, and the final model. The result is a comprehensive view of where 3DGS systems fail, where those failures become detectable, and how to build toward safer deployment.

02 · Engineering

Large-Scale ML Systems & Experimentation

I turn research ideas into large-scale working systems. I decomposed a complex experimental codebase across multiple workstations, integrated a 10TB benchmark, and orchestrated parallel evaluation across 10 GPUs. I focus on making heavy research pipelines scalable, reproducible, and fast enough to iterate on at real research scale.

03 · Personal

Rapid Cross-Domain ML Development

I learn new domains quickly and become productive fast. My research has moved from molecular representation learning to multimodal learning, AI agents, and 3D vision, each requiring a different technical stack and way of thinking. I combine fast prototyping, aggressive iteration, and strong execution with a long-term focus on building trustworthy AI systems that can survive real-world deployment.
